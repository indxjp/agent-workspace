## §2.1 Safety measures before work

Before inspecting or repairing equipment, switch off the main power and apply lockout/tagout
(LOTO). The worker keeps the key and hands it to no one until the work is finished.

<!-- page 4 -->

## §2.3 Residual pressure in hydraulic and pneumatic equipment

Before taking hydraulic or pneumatic equipment apart, release the residual pressure in the circuit,
accumulators included, and confirm that the pressure gauge reads zero. Never skip the residual
pressure check.

<!-- page 7 -->

## §4.2 Protective equipment

Wear safety glasses and oil-resistant gloves for work on hydraulic equipment. Where hot hydraulic
oil may be touched, wear heat-resistant gloves as well.
