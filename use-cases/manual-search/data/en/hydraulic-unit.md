## §3.1 Daily inspection

Before starting work, check visually that the oil level on the gauge is between the upper and lower
lines, that no pipe joint leaks oil, and that there is no abnormal noise or vibration. If anything
is abnormal, do not start operation and contact the Equipment Maintenance Section.

<!-- page 12 -->

## §3.2 Periodic inspection interval

Carry out the periodic inspection every 6 months or every 2,000 operating hours, whichever comes
first. The periodic inspection cleans the suction strainer, checks the pressure gauge calibration
and checks that the relief valve operates. Record the results on form HU-F12 and enter them in the
maintenance ledger.

<!-- page 14 -->

## §3.4 Hydraulic oil replacement

Replace all the hydraulic oil every 4,000 operating hours or every year, whichever comes first.
Replace it at once, whatever the interval, when contamination exceeds NAS class 10 or water exceeds
0.1%. The specified oil is ISO VG46 anti-wear hydraulic oil.

<!-- page 15 -->

## §3.5 Relief valve setting

The relief valve is set to 14.0 MPa. Change the setting only with the design department's approval,
then confirm the measured value on the pressure gauge and record it on form HU-F15.
