(A sample travel and expense policy of the fictional company Hashibiro Shoji Co., Ltd. A summary of the version in force from 2026-04-01. It says nothing about tax treatment.)

## Policy

Domestic lodging is at most JPY 12,000 a night including tax, one line per night. A line above JPY 12,000 without a prior approval number is NG.

## Example fix

Pay the amount above the limit yourself, or resubmit with a prior approval number.
