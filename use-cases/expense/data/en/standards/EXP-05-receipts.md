(A sample travel and expense policy of the fictional company Hashibiro Shoji Co., Ltd. A summary of the version in force from 2026-04-01. It says nothing about tax treatment.)

## Policy

Every line needs a receipt attached, and the amount claimed must equal the receipt amount. A line with no receipt, or with a different amount, is NG.

## Example fix

Attach the receipt and resubmit with the amount on the receipt.
