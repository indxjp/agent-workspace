(A sample travel and expense policy of the fictional company Hashibiro Shoji Co., Ltd. A summary of the version in force from 2026-04-01. It says nothing about tax treatment.)

## Policy

Meeting and entertainment meals need the attendees' names (and the company of anyone from outside). Without attendees it is caution. Entertainment needs a prior request number; without one it is NG.

## Example fix

Add the attendees' names and companies. For entertainment, resubmit with the prior request number.
