(A sample travel and expense policy of the fictional company Hashibiro Shoji Co., Ltd. A summary of the version in force from 2026-04-01. It says nothing about tax treatment.)

## Policy

A taxi is reimbursed only for travel after 22:00 or where there is no public transport. Without a reason (the time or the route) in the remarks it is caution.

## Example fix

Add the time of the ride, or why there was no public transport, to the remarks.
