(A sample travel and expense policy of the fictional company Hashibiro Shoji Co., Ltd. A summary of the version in force from 2026-04-01. It says nothing about tax treatment.)

## Policy

Shinkansen and limited express trains are reimbursed up to an ordinary reserved seat. Green Car or Gran Class without a prior approval number is NG.

## Example fix

Resubmit at the ordinary reserved-seat fare, or add a prior approval number.
