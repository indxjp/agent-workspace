# Privacy impact assessment checklist

When a new service or system handles personal data, check its design memo against each item below and write the assessment. The assessment sets out the facts and the points to check; Legal decides whether it complies with the law.

- PIA-01 Purpose: each item collected has a purpose, within the purposes in our privacy policy.
- PIA-02 Minimum necessary: only what the purpose needs is collected.
- PIA-03 Sensitive information: when health, medical history, beliefs or other sensitive information is collected, how the person's consent is obtained is decided. Allergies count as health information.
- PIA-04 Third parties, outsourcing and transfers abroad: for anything provided or outsourced, the recipient, the data, its country and the contract are decided.
- PIA-05 Retention: the retention period, and how data is deleted when it ends or a member leaves, are decided.
- PIA-06 Security: encryption, who may view the data and access logging are decided.
- PIA-07 Requests from individuals: a contact point and procedure for disclosure, correction, deletion and suspension requests are decided.
- PIA-08 Test data: testing and development use fictional data, never real personal data.
