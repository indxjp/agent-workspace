# Technical memo, part flipping device

## Background

Before the assembly line, operators check by eye which side of each resin part faces up and turn the parts over by hand. We are studying a device that does this automatically, and want to know the closest prior patents before considering a filing.

## Elements

- A: A camera above the belt conveyor photographs the resin parts and judges from the image which side faces up.
- B: Only the parts judged to face down are flipped by compressed air blown from an air nozzle.
- C: The moment to blow is worked out from the part's position given by the conveyor's encoder.
- D: After flipping, a second camera judges each part again, and parts that failed to flip are sent to a separate lane.
