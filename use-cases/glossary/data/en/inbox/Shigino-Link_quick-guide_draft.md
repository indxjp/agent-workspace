# Shigino Link quick guide (draft)

(A fictional sample. It has nothing to do with any real company, product or person. Written by Shigino Keiso Co., Ltd., Support)

## 1. Introduction

This guide is for end users who are new to Shigino Link.

## 2. Log on

Open Shigino Link in a browser, enter your email address and password, and log on. If you forget your password, ask your administrator to reset it.

## 3. The home screen

After you log on, the home screen opens and lists the latest readings of your registered sensors.

## 4. Setting an alarm

Set a trigger level for each sensor, and an alarm is emailed to you when a reading crosses it. Only an administrator can set it.
