# RPT-01 Logistics site consolidation: study report

- Company: Misosazai Foods Co., Ltd. (fictional)
- Prepared by: Logistics Department, site consolidation project (lead: Masumi Uzurahashi)
- Version: 2nd edition (2026-10-28)
- Distribution: management committee members

## 1. Background

Our ambient products ship from three sites: the North, South and Kawabe warehouses. All three are leased, and the leases of the North and South warehouses end at the end of March 2028. The South warehouse is old, and in June 2026 its landlord said they do not intend to renew the lease.

## 2. Current state

2.1 The three sites hold 18,400 pallets in total; the average stock in the first half of FY2026 was 16,900 pallets.

2.2 Logistics costs for ambient products in FY2025 were JPY 2.46 billion (storage JPY 0.98 billion, transfers between sites JPY 0.34 billion, delivery JPY 1.14 billion).

2.3 "Split shipments", where one order ships from more than one site, are 14% of all shipments and the main cause of transfers between sites.

2.4 In the peak season (December), the South warehouse missed the shipping cut-off 9 times in the month (December 2025).

## 3. Options studied

- Option A: keep three sites and lease a nearby warehouse to replace the South warehouse.
- Option B: merge the North and South warehouses into one new logistics center; keep the Kawabe warehouse.
- Option C: merge all three sites into one new logistics center.

## 4. The project's recommendation

We recommend Option B. Option C is not recommended because delivery distances to the Kawabe area would grow and delivery costs are expected to rise. Option A is not recommended because transfers and split shipments would remain.

## 5. Investment and costs (Option B)

5.1 The investment is JPY 4.2 billion (land JPY 1.5 billion, building JPY 2.1 billion, equipment and systems JPY 0.6 billion). The land price is a real-estate agent's rough estimate for the two candidate sites.

5.2 Expected logistics cost savings after start-up: JPY 0.31 billion a year (an estimate, on the assumptions of Appendix A).

5.3 One-off transition costs: JPY 0.22 billion (rent and staff during parallel operation, and the move).

## 6. Schedule (Option B)

- By March 2027: choose and acquire the land
- April 2027 to January 2028: construction and equipment
- February to March 2028: move and parallel operation
- April 2028: the new logistics center in full operation

## 7. Risks

7.1 If the land is acquired later than March 2027, the move cannot finish before the North and South leases end (end of March 2028). A short extension of the South lease cannot be expected, given the landlord's intention.

7.2 Depending on where the new center is, some current warehouse staff may find the commute difficult, and the staff needed may not be secured.

7.3 Migrating the warehouse management system may stop shipping for some days. The migration steps and duration are not yet decided.

7.4 The construction estimate uses unit prices as of September 2026. Rising material prices would increase the investment.

## 8. Open matters

8.1 The final choice of land (two candidate sites).

8.2 What to do with the North warehouse site (return it, or use it for something else).

8.3 Staff assignment and commuting support for warehouse staff (under discussion with HR).
