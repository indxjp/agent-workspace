# Press P-3 die change, walkthrough (transcript)

[00:12] Ukai: Right, the die change on P-3, in the usual order. First the protective gear: safety shoes, protective gloves, safety glasses.

[00:40] Ukai: Before you start, stop the slide just above bottom dead centre and set the mode switch to "inch".

[01:25] Ukai: Then switch off the main power and hang your own padlock and tag on the power panel. The key stays in your pocket. Never skip this.

[02:05] Ukai: Check there is no residual pressure: the pressure gauge must read zero. If it does not, do not touch anything.

[02:50] Ukai: Loosen the clamp bolts of the upper die, then the lower die. Loosen them in turn, diagonally.

[03:40] Ukai: Lift the old die with the crane and lower it onto the trolley. Heavy ones take two people. Never stand under a suspended load.

[05:10] Ukai: Once the new die is in, line it up with the locating pins and tighten the clamp bolts. Tighten to the value in the manual, with a torque wrench.

[06:30] Ukai: When they are tight, check the [inaudible], then take off the lock and switch the power on.

[07:15] Ukai: Bring it down slowly once in inch mode and look at the contact. If it is fine, do three trial strokes.
