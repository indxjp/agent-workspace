# EQ-03 Mechanical press P-3 operating manual (excerpt)

## 6.1 Before a die change

- Stop the slide just before bottom dead centre and set the mode switch to "inch".

## 6.2 Clamp bolts

- The clamp bolts of the upper and lower dies are M16. The tightening torque is 120 N·m.
- Tighten and loosen them diagonally, in several passes.

## 6.3 Checks after the change

- Before removing the lock, check that no bolt is left loose and that the die height display matches the value specified for the die.
- After the trial strokes, send the first part to Quality for inspection.
