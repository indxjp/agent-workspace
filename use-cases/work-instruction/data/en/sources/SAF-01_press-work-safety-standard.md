# SAF-01 Press Work Safety Standard

## 1 Protective equipment

- 1.1 Press work requires safety shoes, protective gloves and safety glasses.
- 1.2 Die setup work also requires a safety helmet.

## 2 Isolating energy (lock and tag)

- 2.1 Before changing, adjusting or cleaning a die, switch off the main power and hang the worker's own padlock and a "Work in progress, do not operate" tag on the power panel.
- 2.2 The worker keeps the key. Never remove someone else's lock.
- 2.3 Start work only after the pneumatic and hydraulic pressure has been released (the gauge reads zero).
- 2.4 Before removing the lock, check that no tool is left behind and that everyone is outside the danger zone.

## 3 Lifting and slinging

- 3.1 Crane lifts are done by a person qualified in slinging.
- 3.2 Never stand under a suspended load.
- 3.3 Anything over 25 kg moved by hand takes two or more people.

## 4 Trial run

- 4.1 The first stroke after a change is made in inch mode, to check the die contact.
- 4.2 During the trial run, start the press only with the two-hand push buttons.
