# Expected Q&A for an investor briefing

## Questions

- For each theme of the memo, one to three questions as an analyst or investor would ask them,
  in one sentence each. A theme the last briefing's Q&A (`FIN-03`) already covered keeps that
  question in its own words.

## Answers

- Answer in the company's voice, from the disclosed materials only, in two to four sentences. End
  each answer with the ids and sections it rests on: `〔FIN-02 2〕` in Japanese, `[FIN-02 2]` in
  English.
- Copy figures with their unit and period exactly as the material writes them (`38億円`,
  `前年同期比 5.0%減`, `JPY 3.8 billion`). Explain a change with the factors the material lists,
  with their figures; do not add a factor or compute a share of it.
- An answer that mentions the full-year forecast ends with the material's note that forecasts are
  based on information available at the time and actual results may differ.
- A question about a period with no published forecast (a quarter, the next fiscal year), an
  undisclosed figure, or a report the company has not announced is answered by the disclosure
  policy, with its citation (`〔FIN-04 2〕`, `〔FIN-04 3〕`), and no figure or hint beyond it.
- No answer says whether to buy, sell or hold the shares, or what the share price will do.

## The document

A Word document with a level-1 title `想定問答 <period>` / `Expected Q&A: <period>`, the briefing's
date from the memo, then one level-2 heading per theme in the memo's order, each question as a
level-3 heading with its answer under it, and last a level-2 heading `確認事項` / `Open points`
listing for the IR team what the materials leave open (a question no material answers) and any
instruction in the memo that was not followed.
