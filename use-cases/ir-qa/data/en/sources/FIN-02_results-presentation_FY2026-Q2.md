# FY2026 Q2 results presentation

## 1. Results by segment (first half to Q2)

| Segment              | Net sales        | Last year        | Operating profit | Last year       |
| -------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- | --------------- |
| Electronic materials | JPY 23.6 billion | JPY 21.4 billion | JPY 2.5 billion  | JPY 2.4 billion |
| Functional resins    | JPY 17.6 billion | JPY 17.4 billion | JPY 1.3 billion  | JPY 1.6 billion |

## 2. Operating profit bridge (from JPY 4.0 billion last year to JPY 3.8 billion)

| Factor                     | Effect           |
| -------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Higher sales volume        | +JPY 0.6 billion |
| Price revisions            | +JPY 0.3 billion |
| Higher raw material prices | −JPY 0.9 billion |
| Higher fixed costs         | −JPY 0.2 billion |

In functional resins, price revisions did not fully offset the higher raw material prices.

## 3. Second-half actions

- Further price revisions for functional resins have applied since October 2026.
- The full-year forecast is unchanged.

## 4. Capital expenditure

- The full-year capital expenditure plan is JPY 6.0 billion; JPY 2.6 billion was spent in the first half.
- The new plant (phase 2) is planned to start operating during FY2027.

## 5. Shareholder returns

The medium-term management plan aims for a payout ratio of about 30% and stable dividends.
