# Disclosure policy

## 1. What we answer

In dialogue with investors, we answer within the information already disclosed. We do not answer about results, deals or figures that have not been disclosed.

## 2. Forward-looking statements

The outlook is explained within the published forecast and its assumptions. No outlook is given for a period that has no published forecast (a single quarter, or the next fiscal year).

## 3. Reports and speculation

We do not comment on reports or speculation about anything the company has not announced.

## 4. Quiet period

From the day after each quarter's closing date until the results are announced, we refrain from answering questions about results.
