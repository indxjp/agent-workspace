## §2.1 Safety measures before inspection or repair

Source: [JS-350 Equipment Manual p.10 §2.1]

Before inspection or repair, stop operation, switch off the main power and apply lockout/tagout
(LOTO). The heating cylinder stays hot after stopping, so wear heat-resistant gloves. Release the
residual pressure in the hydraulic circuit before work.

<!-- page 52 -->

## §7.1 When clamping pressure will not build

Source: [JS-350 Equipment Manual p.52 §7.1]

When clamping pressure will not build, check in order the hydraulic oil quantity, the packing of
the clamping cylinder and the command value of the proportional pressure valve.
