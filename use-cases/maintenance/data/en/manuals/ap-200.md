## §2.1 Safety measures before inspection or repair

Source: [AP-200 Equipment Manual p.12 §2.1]

Before inspection or repair, stop operation and lower the slide to bottom dead centre or insert a
safety block. Switch off the main power and apply lockout/tagout (LOTO); the worker keeps the key.
For work that opens the hydraulic circuit, release the residual pressure, accumulators included,
and confirm that the pressure gauge reads zero.

## §5.3 Cleaning the suction strainer

Source: [AP-200 Equipment Manual p.38 §5.3]

Remove the suction strainer through the inspection hatch on top of the tank. Wash it in cleaning
oil and dry it by blowing air from the inside. Replace it if the mesh is torn. After refitting, run
without load for 5 minutes and confirm that the pump makes no abnormal noise.

## §6.2 Inspection order when pressure will not build

Source: [AP-200 Equipment Manual p.41 §6.2]

When pressure will not build, inspect in this order. (1) Check the oil quantity on the oil gauge.
(2) Listen for abnormal pump noise. (3) Check the suction strainer for clogging (§5.3). (4) Check
the relief valve's operation and debris caught in it (§6.4). (5) Check the coupling for wear
(§6.5). (6) Check that the unloading solenoid valve is energised and measure its coil resistance.
If the cause is still not found, stop the work and contact the person in charge of the
Maintenance Section.

<!-- page 43 -->

## §6.4 Relief valve

Source: [AP-200 Equipment Manual p.43 §6.4]

The relief valve is set to 21.0 MPa. Do not change the setting. If debris is suspected, take the
valve apart and clean it, then after reassembly confirm the measured value on the pressure gauge
and record it on form AP-F21.

<!-- page 44 -->

## §6.5 Coupling inspection

Source: [AP-200 Equipment Manual p.44 §6.5]

Check the rubber spider of the coupling between the motor and the pump for cracks or chips. Inspect
with the main power off and the coupling cover removed. Replace it if worn and check the alignment.
