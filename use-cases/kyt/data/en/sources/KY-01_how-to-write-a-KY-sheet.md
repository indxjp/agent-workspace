# KY-01 How to write a KY sheet (Sekirei Kogyo, Health and Safety Committee)

At the KY (hazard prediction) meeting before the work, the team raises the hazards together and decides the measures and the action goal. The team leader prepares a draft of the sheet.

1. Round 1 "What hazards are there?": for each scene of the work, write each hazard as "because ..., when ..., ... happens".
2. Round 2 "These are the key hazards": the team picks the most important ones from round 1 at the meeting. The draft picks none.
3. Round 3 "What would you do?": write a concrete measure for each hazard.
4. Round 4 "This is what we will do": the team decides its action goal and the words for pointing and calling at the meeting. The draft decides none.
5. The draft names the basis of each hazard and measure (the work standard's item, the near-miss report's number).
6. At the end of the sheet, write when, where, the task and the members, and when the meeting is.
