# SAF-02 Forklift work standard (Sekirei Kogyo, Health and Safety Committee)

Revised: 2026-04-01

1. Only people who completed the forklift operator course drive a forklift.
2. Before the day's work, the driver does the pre-use check (brakes, forks, warning devices) and fills in the check sheet.
3. Racking and picking from racks need a banksman. The banksman stands where the driver can see them and guides with the agreed signals.
4. The banksman never enters the area behind the forklift or its turning circle.
5. Nobody rides on the forks or on a pallet.
6. Never travel with the load raised. When travelling, lower the forks to 15 to 20 cm above the floor.
7. Drive slowly on a wet floor. On rainy days, lay non-slip mats at the loading entrance.
8. Before putting a pallet into a rack, check the pallet for damage and check that the slot in the rack is clear.
9. Stop where the route crosses a pedestrian walkway, and look both ways.
10. Workers wear a helmet and safety shoes.
