# Donation Rules

## §2.1 Checking the recipient

General Affairs checks that the recipient is not an antisocial group and the ringi records the result.

## §2.2 Prohibited donations

No donation is made to a political organisation, an individual politician or a religious body, nor
any donation that could be taken as a reward for business.

## §2.3 Annual limit

Donations to one recipient are limited to 3,000,000 yen per fiscal year.
