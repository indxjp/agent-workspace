# Ringi Rules

## §4.1 Required entries

A ringi states its subject, category, amount (excluding tax), department, drafter, purpose, expected
benefit, payment terms, planned date and approval route. A ringi missing any of them is not
accepted and goes back to its department.

## §4.2 Quotes attached

Capital investment, outsourcing and IT investment over 1,000,000 yen attach quotes from two or more
suppliers. When only one supplier is asked, the ringi states the reason for a single supplier.

## §4.4 Approval route stated

The drafter states the approval route (approver and co-review) according to the schedule of the
Authority Rules. A ringi whose stated route differs from the schedule goes back to its department
before it is circulated.

## §5.1 No early start

No contract, order or work may start before the ringi is approved.

## §5.2 Budget

A ringi outside the budget says so and names its source of funds, and is co-reviewed by Corporate
Planning.
