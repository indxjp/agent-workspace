# Mizunara Kintai administrator guide

## 1. Administrator password

- When you forget the password, use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in screen to send a reset email to your registered email address. The link in the email is valid for 30 minutes.
- When the reset email does not arrive, check your spam folder. When it still does not arrive, contact the support desk (GUIDE-03).
- Five wrong passwords in a row lock the account for 15 minutes. Sign in again after 15 minutes, or reset the password to unlock it.

## 2. Monthly CSV export

- Attendance data is exported as CSV, one month at a time, from "Monthly CSV export" in the "Reports" menu.
- "Column settings" changes which columns are exported and their order.
- The character encoding can be UTF-8 or Shift_JIS.

## 3. Clocking in on a smartphone, and location

- Location recording is turned on or off by an administrator under "Settings" > "Clock-in". It is off by default.
- Only the location at the moment of clocking in is recorded; movement during work is not.
