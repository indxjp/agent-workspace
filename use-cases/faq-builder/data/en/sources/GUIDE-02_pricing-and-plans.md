# Mizunara Kintai pricing and plans

## 1. Plans

| Plan     | Monthly fee (per person, excl. tax) |
| -------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Standard | JPY 300                             |
| Premium  | JPY 500                             |

## 2. Free trial

- Every feature is free for 14 days from sign-up.
- The trial does not turn into a paid plan by itself: use stops until you choose a plan.

## 3. Adding employees in the middle of a month

- One person's monthly fee is added from the month you add them. There is no pro rata calculation.

## 4. Cancelling

- You can use the service until the last day of the month you cancel in. Monthly fees already paid are not refunded.
