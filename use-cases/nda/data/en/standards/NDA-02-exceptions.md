(A fictional sample standard.)

## Standard

All five exceptions are present: (1) public when disclosed; (2) became public after disclosure through no fault of the recipient; (3) already held by the recipient; (4) received from a third party with the right to disclose it and without a duty of confidence; (5) developed independently without the confidential information. If any one is missing, information the company develops itself could be bound: mark it NG.

## Standard clause

Confidential Information does not include information that: (1) was public when disclosed; (2) became public after disclosure through no fault of the recipient; (3) the recipient already held when it was disclosed; (4) the recipient received from a third party with the right to disclose it and without a duty of confidence; or (5) the recipient developed independently without using the Confidential Information.
