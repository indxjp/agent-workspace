## Standard

The agreement runs 2 years from signing, and the confidentiality obligations survive 3 years after it ends. Obligations that survive "without time limit" never stop needing management: mark it caution and propose 3 years. No survival clause at all is also caution.

## Standard clause

This agreement is effective for 2 years from the date it is signed. The confidentiality obligations in this agreement survive for 3 years after it ends.
