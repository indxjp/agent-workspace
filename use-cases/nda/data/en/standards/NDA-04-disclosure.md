(A fictional sample standard.)

## Standard

Disclosure to a third party needs the other party's prior written consent, except to officers and employees who need to know for the purpose and to lawyers, accountants and other advisers under a legal duty of confidence, each bound by equivalent obligations. A clause that does not allow disclosure even to officers and employees is caution. A clause that allows officers and employees is OK even without the advisers.

## Standard clause

The recipient shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the discloser's prior written consent, except to its officers and employees who need to know it for the Purpose, and to lawyers, certified public accountants and other advisers under a legal duty of confidence, each bound by obligations equivalent to this agreement.
