---

(A fictional sample standard.)

## Standard

Confidential information is limited to information disclosed with a confidential marking, and information disclosed orally and identified as confidential in writing within 30 days. A clause that covers unmarked information, such as "all information disclosed", widens what must be managed too far: mark it caution and propose the standard clause.

## Standard clause

Confidential Information means information the discloser discloses to the recipient in writing or electronically with a clear confidential marking, and information disclosed orally that the discloser identifies as confidential in writing within 30 days of disclosure.
