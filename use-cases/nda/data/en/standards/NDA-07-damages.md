## Standard

Damages are limited to the ordinary damages a breach actually causes the other party. A fixed penalty, or a penalty plus further damages, is NG: propose the standard clause.

## Standard clause

If the recipient breaches this agreement and causes the discloser loss, the recipient shall compensate the ordinary damages the discloser actually suffers as a result.
