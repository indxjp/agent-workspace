# Your first day

## The day

| Time | What | Where |
| ---- | ---- | ----- |
| 8:45 | Reception. Collect your building pass | Head office, 1st floor reception |
| 9:00 | Joining formalities (hand in documents, the work rules explained) | Head office, 2nd floor, Meeting room 1 |
| 10:30 | Collect your laptop and set up your accounts (ONB-02) | Head office, 2nd floor, IT section |
| 12:00 | Lunch (the staff canteen is open to you) | Head office, 1st floor |
| 13:00 | Go to your team. Your manager and mentor introduce the team | Your team |
| 17:00 | End of the day | |

## What to bring

- Proof of identity (a driving licence, for example)
- The salary account form (the form we emailed you beforehand)
- Something to write with

## What to wear

Your own clothes are fine on the first day. On days you enter the plant, we lend you work clothes and safety shoes (ONB-04).
