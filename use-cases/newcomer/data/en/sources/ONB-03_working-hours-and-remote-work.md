# Working hours and remote work

## Working hours

- We work flexible hours, with core time from 10:00 to 15:00.
- Clock in and out every day on the "Attendance" screen of the company portal.

## Remote work

- For the first month from your start date you do not work remotely: you come to the office every day to learn the work.
- After that, with your manager's approval, you can work remotely up to 2 days a week.

## Taking a day off

Tell your manager by chat or phone before your working day starts, and apply for the leave on the attendance screen.
