# First-week training

## The standard plan

| Day | Morning | Afternoon |
| --- | ------- | --------- |
| Day 1 | Joining formalities, collect your laptop (ONB-01) | Your team introduced |
| Day 2 | Company induction (the business, the work rules) | Information security e-learning |
| Day 3 | Your team's work explained (ONB-05) | Shadow your mentor |
| Day 4 | Plant tour (with safety training) | Shadow your mentor |
| Day 5 | Shadow your mentor | Review with your manager and mentor |

## Please note

- Finish the information security e-learning by the end of Day 5.
- On the plant tour day we lend you work clothes and safety shoes. Tell your mentor your sizes by Day 3.
- Experienced hires follow the standard plan too.
