# About Quality Assurance

## The department

- Quality Assurance has two sections, Quality Control and Quality Planning. The head of the department is Chihiro Washimi.
- Quality Control inspects products before shipping and answers customers' quality questions.
- Quality Planning owns the quality targets and the internal audits.

## Regular meetings

- Department meeting: every Monday, 9:30 to 10:00 (head office, 3rd floor, Quality Assurance meeting area)
- Section morning meeting: every day, 8:50 to 9:00

## Tools you will use

- Enter inspection records on the "Inspection records" screen of the company portal.
- Report a defect to Quality Planning with the "Defect report" form.
