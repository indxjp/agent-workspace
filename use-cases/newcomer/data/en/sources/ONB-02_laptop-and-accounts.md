# Laptop and accounts

## Setting up on day one

- The IT section hands you your initial password on paper on day one. Change it to your own password the same day.
- Set up the multi-factor authentication app on a company smartphone or your own.
- The IT section connects you to the office Wi-Fi. The Wi-Fi password is never written in a document or a chat.

## If you forget your password

Ask for a reset through the "Help desk" form on the company portal, or call the IT section (extension 2150). They answer on weekdays from 9:00 to 17:30.

## Never

- Tell anyone your password, or write it on a sticky note
- Plug a personal USB stick into a company laptop
