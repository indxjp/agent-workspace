# Answers and the first-week guide

## Answers

- The conclusion in one or two sentences, then each fact as a bullet ending with the document's id:
  `〔ONB-03〕` in Japanese, `[ONB-03]` in English. The information sheet is cited as `〔入社者情報〕` /
  `[Joiner sheet]`.
- A rule without its condition is a different rule: keep "with your manager's approval", a
  deadline or an exception that the same section gives.
- When no document answers the question, answer with this sentence and nothing else:
  - ja: 「入社資料に該当する記載が見つかりませんでした。メンターか人事総務部に確認してください。」
  - en: "The onboarding documents do not cover this. Please ask your mentor or HR and General Affairs."

## The guide

A Word document with one level-1 heading per section, in this order. Every point ends with the id it
rests on.

1. **はじめに / Welcome.** The joiner's name, team, start date, mentor and manager, from the
   information sheet only.
2. **初日の流れ / Your first day.** The times, places and what happens, as a table from `ONB-01`,
   and what to bring.
3. **今週の予定 / Your week.** A table with one row per day (`1日目` / `Day 1` to `5日目` / `Day 5`)
   and the morning and afternoon from `ONB-04`. Day 1 is the start date; do not work out the other
   dates.
4. **準備と期限 / To do this week.** The deadlines and tasks the documents name: changing the
   initial password (`ONB-02`), the e-learning, telling the mentor the shoe and clothes sizes
   (`ONB-04`).
5. **チームのこと / Your team.** The team, its regular meetings and the tools, from `ONB-05`.
6. **働き方 / Working hours.** Core time, clocking in, remote work and taking a day off, from
   `ONB-03`.
7. **困ったときは / Who to ask.** The mentor, the IT section's help desk and extension (`ONB-02`),
   and HR and General Affairs.

No secret (a password, a Wi-Fi key) goes in the guide: say only who sets it up.
