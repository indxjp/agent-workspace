# TM-03 Fees and cancellation change memo

## Articles to change

- Article 5 (Fees)
- Article 8 (Cancellation)

## Revised wording

- Article 5: "The Premium plan costs JPY 580 a month (incl. tax) on the monthly plan, or JPY 5,800 a year (incl. tax) on the annual plan. The monthly plan is billed on the 1st of each month for the previous month; the annual plan is billed for one year on the day of sign-up."
- Article 8: "Users may cancel at any time from the app's settings screen. A month cancelled part-way is not charged pro rata. If the annual plan is cancelled part-way through its term, the fee for the rest of the term is not refunded."

## Reason

- Article 5: server and running costs have risen, so the monthly fee changes, and an annual plan is added.
- Article 8: with the new annual plan, the terms set how cancelling part-way through is handled.
