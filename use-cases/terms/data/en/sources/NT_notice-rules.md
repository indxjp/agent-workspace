(A sample of the in-house rules of a fictional company, Nobitaki Inc.)

# In-house rules for terms update notices

- NT-01: State the notice date and the date the revised terms take effect.
- NT-02: For each article that changes, say in one sentence what changes and how.
- NT-03: State any change that is worse for users (a fee increase, a new no-refund clause) plainly at the start of the notice. Never describe the update differently from what it does, such as "only changes in your favour".
- NT-04: State where the revised terms are shown (the "Terms" screen in the app).
- NT-05: State the contact points ("Contact us" in the app, and the support address support@nobitaki.example).
