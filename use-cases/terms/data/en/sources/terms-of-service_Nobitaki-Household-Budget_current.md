# Nobitaki Household Budget Terms of Service (excerpt)

## Article 1 (Purpose)

These terms set out the conditions for using Nobitaki Household Budget (the "Service"), a household budget app provided by Nobitaki Inc. (the "Company").

## Article 5 (Fees)

The Premium plan costs JPY 480 a month (incl. tax), billed on the 1st of each month for the previous month.

## Article 8 (Cancellation)

Users may cancel at any time from the app's settings screen. A month cancelled part-way is not charged pro rata.

## Article 11 (Points)

Users receive points for keeping their budget record day after day. Points expire one year after they are given.

## Article 15 (Changes to these terms)

When the Company changes these terms, it announces the change and its effective date in the app's notices at least one month before the effective date.
