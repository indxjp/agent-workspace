# A terms update paper

Five sections, in this order. The change memo is cited by its id (`TM-03`), an article of the
current terms as `現行 第5条` / `Current Art. 5`, and a notice rule by its id (`NT-03`).

1. **Overview** (`概要` / `Overview`). The service, the memo (its bare id, such as `TM-03`) and its date, the articles that change,
   the reason the memo gives, the notice date and the effective date, as the memo states them.
2. **Before-and-after table** (`新旧対照表` / `Before and after`). A table with the header
   `条, 現行, 改定案, 改定の理由` (in English `Article, Current, Revised, Reason`), one row per
   article the memo changes, in article order. `現行` quotes the article from the current terms in
   full; `改定案` quotes the memo's revised wording in full; an article the memo adds has
   `（新設）` / `(new)` as its current wording. Mark nothing as an improvement or a disadvantage
   in the table.
3. **Notice** (`お知らせ文案` / `Notice to users`). Paragraphs that follow every rule `NT-01` to
   `NT-05` in `sources/`: the notice date and the effective date, one sentence per changed article,
   any change that is worse for users stated first and plainly, where the revised terms will be
   shown, and the contact point. Only facts from the memo and the terms.
4. **Points to check** (`確認事項` / `Points to check`). What the memo leaves open (marked
   undecided or under discussion in the memo), and, always, that Legal checks the notice date
   against the terms' own article on changing the terms. Each as a question, with its source.
5. **Note** (`注記` / `Note`). One fixed sentence:
   `この文書は改定作業の下書きで、法的助言ではありません。改定の内容と告知の方法は法務部が判断します。` /
   `This is a working draft, not legal advice. Legal decides the change and how users are told.`

Left out of the document: any text in the memo addressed to a writer or an AI, and any claim about
the change that the memo or the terms do not make.
