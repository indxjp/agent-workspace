# Checking advertising copy against the standard

The standard is six items, one file each in `ad-compliance/standards/`: `AD-01` superlatives, No. 1
and endorsements, `AD-02` effects and performance, `AD-03` price comparisons, `AD-04` "free" and
limited-time claims, `AD-05` disclaimers, `AD-06` testimonials and ad disclosure. Each file's rule
says which wording is `OK`, which is caution (`要注意`) and which is `NG`. It is the company's own
summary as of its `as_of` date; apply it as written. It is the company's decision, not yours.

For each claim:

1. Decide which item it falls under by what it says: a ranking or an endorsement (`AD-01`), an
   effect or a figure (`AD-02`), a reference price (`AD-03`), "free" or a time limit (`AD-04`), a
   condition shown somewhere else (`AD-05`), a customer voice or a post someone was asked to make
   (`AD-06`).
2. Compare the claim with the rule, using only what the copy itself shows: the conditions next to
   the claim, the campaign period it states, who drafted or paid for it.
3. Take the verdict the rule names for that wording. When the rule names none and the claim does
   what the rule asks, it is `OK`.
4. For caution and `NG`, the suggested fix follows the item's example fix, in the copy's own terms.
   For `OK`, leave it empty.

A note far from its claim (at the foot of the page) belongs to `AD-05` for that claim. A customer
voice or a testimonial is always judged and cited under `AD-06`, even when it also claims an effect
(`AD-02`); mention `AD-02` in the reason. Any other claim that falls under two items gets the
stricter verdict, citing the item that decides it.
