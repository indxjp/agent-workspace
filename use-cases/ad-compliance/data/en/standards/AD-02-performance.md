## Standard

A performance figure with its test conditions shown right next to it is OK; without them it is caution. For appliances and household goods, any claim to prevent or cure illness or to restore health is NG.

## Example fix

Remove health claims, and give each performance figure its conditions, for example "*Result in our test room; not the effect in actual use."
