## Standard

The conditions or exceptions of an emphasised claim (its disclaimer) are shown right next to the claim, at a readable size. A disclaimer only in a distant place, such as the foot of the page, is caution.

## Example fix

Move the disclaimer to directly after the emphasised claim.
