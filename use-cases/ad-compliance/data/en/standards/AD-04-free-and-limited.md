(A sample in-house standard of the fictional company Rurikake Denki Co., Ltd. It is a snapshot, dated 2026-10-01, that summarises published advertising-claim guidance for internal use. It is not the text or an interpretation of any law.)

## Standard

"Free" or "free shipping" with its conditions shown right next to it is OK. A time limit such as "today only" or "now only" that does not match the actual campaign period is NG.

## Example fix

State the actual campaign period, for example "Campaign: 2026-12-01 to 2026-12-31".
