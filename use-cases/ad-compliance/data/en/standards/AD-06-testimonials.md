(A sample in-house standard of the fictional company Rurikake Denki Co., Ltd. It is a snapshot, dated 2026-10-01, that summarises published advertising-claim guidance for internal use. It is not the text or an interpretation of any law.)

## Standard

A testimonial that shows an effect or benefit is NG, even with "individual opinion" added. A post by a third party the company asked to post is NG without a label such as "Ad" or "PR".

## Example fix

Replace a testimonial that claims an effect with one about how the product feels to use. Put "#PR" or "Ad" at the start of a commissioned post.
