## Standard

A reference price such as "regular price" or "list price" may be used only when the product was recently sold at that price for a reasonable period and the period is stated. Without the period or the sales record it is caution: ask the responsible team to confirm the sales record. A comparison with a price it was never sold at is NG.

## Example fix

State the period, for example "regular price (our selling price from 2026-09-01 to 2026-11-30)". If the sales record cannot be shown, remove the reference price.
