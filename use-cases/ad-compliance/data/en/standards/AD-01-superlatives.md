## Standard

Claims such as "No. 1", "the best", "top in Japan" or "recommended by doctors" may be used only when the same screen states who ran the survey, when and over what, and the company holds the evidence. Such a claim without its basis is NG.

## Example fix

If the basis cannot be shown, remove the claim. If it can, add "according to <survey> (<surveyor>, <period>, <scope>)" on the same screen.
