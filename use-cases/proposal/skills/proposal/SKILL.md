# A sales proposal outline

One slide per section, in this order. Every bullet ends with the id it rests on: a requirement
(`R-01`), a product (`PRD-01`), a price item (`P-01` from `PRICE-01`), the plan (`PLAN-01`) or a case
(`CASE-01`). The meeting notes are cited by their date (`2026-11-18 メモ` / `2026-11-18 notes`).

1. **Title.** The proposal's name, the customer's name, the seller's name and the date of the next
   meeting as the notes give it. No bullets beyond these.
2. **Problem** (`現状の課題`). How the customer works today and what goes wrong, from the meeting
   notes only, in their words.
3. **Requirements** (`ご要望`). One bullet per `R-xx`, with its priority (`必須` / `Must`,
   `希望` / `Want`).
4. **Proposal** (`ご提案`). For each requirement, the product feature that meets it, from the
   `PRD-xx` sheets. A requirement no product meets, or meets only in part, says so (a product
   sheet's "できないこと" / "What it does not do" counts). Propose an option (`PRD-02`) only for a
   requirement that asks for it.
5. **Plan** (`導入計画`). The steps of `PLAN-01`, fitted to the customer's own scope from the notes
   (the trial line, for example). Keep `PLAN-01`'s durations; add none of your own.
6. **Case studies** (`導入事例`). The cases whose products or industry match the customer, at most
   three, each as: the customer, the industry, what they used, and what the customer agreed may be
   quoted, in the case's own words. Prefer the same industry. A case from another industry is
   shown only when no closer one covers a requirement.
7. **Cost** (`費用`). A table with the header `品目, 単位, 単価（円・税抜）, 数量` /
   `Item, Unit, Unit price (JPY excl. tax), Quantity`: one row per `P-xx` the proposal uses, its unit
   price as a number from `PRICE-01`, and the quantity the notes give, or `要確認` /
   `to be confirmed`. No total, no discount.
8. **Open points** (`確認事項`). What the notes leave open (the machine count, a date) and any
   requirement the proposal does not fully meet.

A slide holds at most 20 bullets and its table at most 15 rows (the `office-writer` limits).
