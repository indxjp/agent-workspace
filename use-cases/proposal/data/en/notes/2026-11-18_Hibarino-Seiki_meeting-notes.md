# Hibarino Seiki Co., Ltd. first meeting

## Where they are today

- Daily inspections of the cutting machines at the head plant (about 40 machines) are done on paper
  checklists.
- At month end the Maintenance Section collects the checklists and types them into a spreadsheet by
  hand.
- When a checklist records a fault, Maintenance sometimes only notices it after collection.
- Their core system (production management) can export the equipment register as CSV. They would
  like inspection results to go back as CSV too.

## What they asked for (details in the requirements list)

- Record inspections on tablets.
- Tell the Maintenance Section the same day when a fault is recorded.
- See part replacement timing as a plan.
- Try it first on one line at the head plant, and roll out to every line if it works.

## Decisions and actions

- Bring a proposal to the next meeting (early December). Structure: problem, proposal, plan, cases,
  cost.
- Cost is for the number of machines on the trial line. Ms Itoga will send the number later (not
  yet received).
- They would like to see a case from the same industry.
