# PLAN-01 How a rollout runs (standard)

1. Preparation (2 weeks): import the equipment register CSV and build the checklists from the paper
   ones.
2. Trial (1 month): record inspections on one line, and review the notification recipients and the
   checklist items.
3. Review: go over the trial's results with the customer and decide whether to roll out to every
   line.
4. Rollout: register the machines of the remaining lines and train the staff.

The trial's length and scope are agreed with the customer.
