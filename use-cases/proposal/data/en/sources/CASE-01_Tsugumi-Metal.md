# CASE-01 Tsugumi Metal Industries Co., Ltd.

## Before

month end.

## What they use

PRD-01 Equipment Inspection App and PRD-03 Data Exchange. They tried it on one line first, then
rolled it out to the whole plant.

## What the customer agreed we may quote

- The copying work is gone.
- A recorded fault now reaches the maintenance staff on the day it is recorded.
