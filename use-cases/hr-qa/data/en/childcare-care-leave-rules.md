# Childcare and Family Care Leave Rules

## Chapter 2 Childcare leave

### Article 3 (Who may take it)

1. An employee raising a child under 1 year old may apply for childcare leave.
2. Under the labour-management agreement, the following employees are excluded.
   1. Employees who joined less than 1 year ago
   2. Employees whose employment will clearly end within 1 year of the application
   3. Employees whose scheduled working days are 2 or fewer a week

### Article 4 (Period)

1. Childcare leave lasts until the day the child turns 1 year old.
2. When the child cannot enter a nursery or for a similar reason, it may be extended until the child turns 1 year 6 months, and, when the same reason remains, until the child turns 2.

### Article 5 (How to apply)

1. To take childcare leave, submit the childcare leave application to the HR Department at least 1 month before the planned first day of leave.

## Chapter 3 Family care

### Article 10 (Family care days)

1. To care for a family member in need of care or go with them to hospital, an employee may take 5 days a year of family care leave for one such family member, and 10 days a year for two or more.
2. Family care days may be taken by the hour.

## Chapter 4 While on leave

### Article 12 (Pay while on leave)

1. No salary is paid during childcare leave or family care leave.
2. Social insurance contributions during leave follow the Social Insurance and Benefits Guide.
