# Social Insurance and Benefits Guide

## 1. Health insurance and pension

Employees join the Yoshikiri Seisakusho Health Insurance Society's health insurance and the employees' pension on the day they join. The HR Department handles the procedure.

## 2. Registering dependants

1. To add a family member to your health insurance as a dependant, submit the dependant change form to the HR Department within 5 days of the event (a marriage, a birth or the like).
2. Attach the following to the dependant change form.
   - A document showing the family relationship (a copy of the residence certificate or the like)
   - A document showing the family member's income (an income certificate or the like)
3. Whether a family member is accepted as a dependant is decided by the health insurance society. The HR Department checks the documents and submits them to the society.

## 3. Celebration and condolence payments

1. The company pays the following.

| Event                                         | Amount     |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| The employee's own marriage                   | JPY 30,000 |
| The birth of a child                          | JPY 20,000 |
| The employee's hospital stay (7 days or more) | JPY 10,000 |

2. Apply within 3 months of the event by submitting the payment application to the HR Department.

## 4. Social insurance contributions while on leave

During childcare leave, the HR Department applies for the exemption from health insurance and pension contributions when the employee asks.
