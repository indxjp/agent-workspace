# Leave Rules

## Chapter 2 Annual paid leave

### Article 5 (Granting annual paid leave)

1. Annual paid leave is granted by length of continuous service, as follows.

| Continuous service       | Days granted |
| ------------------------ | ------------ |
| 6 months                 | 10 days      |
| 1 year 6 months          | 11 days      |
| 2 years 6 months         | 12 days      |
| 3 years 6 months         | 14 days      |
| 4 years 6 months         | 16 days      |
| 5 years 6 months         | 18 days      |
| 6 years 6 months or more | 20 days      |

### Article 6 (How to take it)

1. To take annual paid leave, request it in the attendance system as a rule at least 3 business days before the day, and get the manager's approval.
2. Annual paid leave may also be taken in half days: a morning half day is 9:00 to 13:00, an afternoon half day 14:00 to 18:00.
3. When a sudden illness or another unavoidable reason prevents a request in advance, request it within 2 business days of returning to work.

### Article 7 (Carry-over)

1. Annual paid leave not taken in a year may be carried over to the next year only.

## Chapter 3 Special leave

### Article 9 (Leave for family events)

1. On request, special leave (paid) is granted in the following cases.

| Event                              | Days   |
| ---------------------------------- | ------ |
| The employee's own marriage        | 5 days |
| The spouse giving birth            | 3 days |
| Death of a spouse, child or parent | 5 days |
| Death of a grandparent or sibling  | 2 days |

2. Special leave is taken within 1 month of the day of the event.

### Article 11 (Sick leave)

1. An employee absent for 4 or more consecutive days through their own illness or injury submits a doctor's certificate to the HR Department and may take up to 10 days a year of paid sick leave.
