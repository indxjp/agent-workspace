title: Unit price
---

(A sample invoice-matching rule of the fictional company Shigi Seiki Co., Ltd. Purchasing, revised 2026-04-01. It says nothing about accounting or tax treatment.)

## Rule

An invoice unit price equal to the order's unit price is OK. Any other unit price is NG.

## Action

Ask the supplier to reissue at the order's unit price. If a price change was agreed, Purchasing corrects the order.
