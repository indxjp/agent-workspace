title: Item on the order
---

(A sample invoice-matching rule of the fictional company Shigi Seiki Co., Ltd. Purchasing, revised 2026-04-01. It says nothing about accounting or tax treatment.)

## Rule

An invoice line whose part number is on the purchase order the invoice names is OK. A part number not on that order (shipping, fees and the like included) is NG.

## Action

For a line not on the order, ask the supplier to reissue the invoice without it, or have Purchasing confirm an additional order.
