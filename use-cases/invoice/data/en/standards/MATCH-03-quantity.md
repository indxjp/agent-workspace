title: Three-way quantity match
---

(A sample invoice-matching rule of the fictional company Shigi Seiki Co., Ltd. Purchasing, revised 2026-04-01. It says nothing about accounting or tax treatment.)

## Rule

An invoiced quantity that is no more than the ordered quantity and equal to the accepted quantity on the delivery note is OK. An invoiced quantity above the accepted quantity is NG. One below it is caution: check whether it is a partial invoice.

## Action

Ask the supplier to reissue for the accepted quantity. For caution, ask the supplier whether the rest will be invoiced separately.
