(A sample invoice-matching rule of the fictional company Shigi Seiki Co., Ltd. Purchasing, revised 2026-04-01. It says nothing about accounting or tax treatment.)

## Rule

An invoice line is OK when the order number is on the line or in the invoice header and the delivery note number is on the line. A line with no order number anywhere, or with no delivery note number, is caution.

## Action

Confirm the missing number with the supplier and add it to the matching record.
