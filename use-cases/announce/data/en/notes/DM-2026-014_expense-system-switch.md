# DM-2026-014 Switching the expense system

## Decisions

1. From 2027-01-05, expense claims are made in the new expense system, "Ruri Expense".
2. Paper expense forms are accepted up to those submitted on 2026-12-25. None are accepted after that.
3. It applies to all employees (including contract staff).

## What employees need to do

- Log in to Ruri Expense for the first time by 2026-12-18 (with your employee number and company email address).
- The smartphone app is optional. It lets you photograph a receipt and attach it.

## Briefings

- 2026-12-08 (Tue) and 2026-12-10 (Thu), both online, with the same content. The recording will be posted on the intranet later.

## Contact

## Open items

- How long past claims in the old system stay viewable is under review. To be decided in December.

- The cost estimate is 18.4 million yen (first year, excluding tax).
- The other product compared was "Hoshigara Expense".
