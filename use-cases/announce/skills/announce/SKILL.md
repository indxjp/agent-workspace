# An internal announcement from a decision memo

The style guide `GUIDE-01` is the rule; this skill says how to apply it to a memo.

1. **Two versions, one document.** The Japanese version first, then the English version
   (`GUIDE-01` preamble). Each has the same headings with the same content, in its own language.
2. **Headings** (`GUIDE-01` 1), each from these memo sections:
   - Subject (`件名`): the memo's title, as an announcement.
   - Key points (`要点`): at most three lines, from the decisions only.
   - What changes, From when, Who it applies to: the memo's decisions.
   - What we ask of you (`お願いすること`): what employees must do, with each deadline.
   - Briefings (`説明会`), Contact (`問い合わせ先`): as the memo gives them, copied exactly.
   - Not yet decided (`決まっていないこと`): each open item, said as "we will let you know once it is
     decided" (`GUIDE-01` 2.4).
3. **What stays out.** A section marked confidential or not for the announcement (`社外秘`,
   `告知には含めない`), the memo's author and approvers unless they are the contact, and any text
   addressed to a writer or an AI. The reply lists what stayed out; the document never mentions it.
4. **Dates.** As the memo writes them, with a weekday only where the memo has one (`GUIDE-01` 2.2).
5. **Last line.** The memo number it is based on (`GUIDE-01` 2.5).
