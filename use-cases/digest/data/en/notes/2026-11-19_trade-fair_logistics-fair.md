# Trade fair notes: the Fictional Logistics Fair

- Mobile robots that run in freezer warehouses stood out. At Tobishima Robotics' booth we saw a
  model not yet on sale.
- Most automated storage on show moved whole shelves.
- Every booth advised measuring aisle widths and floor steps before installing.
