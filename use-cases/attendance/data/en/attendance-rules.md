# Attendance check rules

## Missed clock-ins

A missed clock-in is a day whose day type is "work" with the clock-in or the clock-out left blank. A day of another type (paid leave, sick leave, special leave and the like) needs no punches.

## Overtime

Under the labour agreement, overtime is limited to 45 hours a month. Anyone whose month-to-date total in the monthly summary is 36 hours or more is "nearing the limit", and anyone over 45 hours is "over the limit"; tell their manager.

## How to tell

For each manager, show the people concerned, the dates and the rows of the timeclock export or the monthly summary. The attendance check is a prompt to correct punches or adjust workloads; it is never used to rate or discipline anyone. Never write the reason for a leave, or someone's health or other private matters.
