(A sample inspection standard of the fictional company Yoshikiri Metal Co., Ltd. Part BK-210 (mounting bracket), revision C, revised 2026-04-01.)

## Specification

44.90 to 45.10 mm (caliper)

## Rule

If every sample is between 44.90 and 45.10 mm, it is OK. If any sample is outside, it is NG. A blank measurement is caution. A value exactly at the lower or upper limit is within the limits.

## Action

For NG, put the lot on shipping hold; Quality Assurance decides the disposition (100% sort, rework or scrap). For caution, re-measure the blank sample and record it.
