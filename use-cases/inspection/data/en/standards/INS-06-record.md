(A sample inspection standard of the fictional company Yoshikiri Metal Co., Ltd. Part BK-210 (mounting bracket), revision C, revised 2026-04-01.)

## Specification

Each inspection row records the instrument number, inspector, checker and inspection date.

## Rule

If every row has the instrument number, inspector, checker and inspection date, it is OK. Any blank is caution.

## Action

The inspector or the checker fills in the blank field.
