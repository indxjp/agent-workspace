(A sample inspection standard of the fictional company Yoshikiri Metal Co., Ltd. Part BK-210 (mounting bracket), revision C, revised 2026-04-01.)

## Specification

No scratches, burrs or rust (visual). Mark "good" or "defect".

## Rule

If every sample is marked "good", it is OK. If any sample is marked "defect", it is NG. A blank mark is caution.

## Action

For NG, isolate the defective sample; Quality Assurance decides the disposition. For caution, repeat the visual check and record it.
