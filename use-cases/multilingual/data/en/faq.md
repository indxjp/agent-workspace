# Customer support FAQ (customers abroad)

The FAQ the support desk of Komorebi Goods, a fictional online shop, uses to answer enquiries from
abroad. A reply says only what is written here.

## FAQ-SHIP-01 Delivery times abroad and tracking

- Orders abroad are sent by international post. Delivery usually takes 7 to 14 business days from
  dispatch. We cannot promise an arrival date.
- While a parcel is in customs, the tracking page can show "In transit" without change for a few
  days.
- When a parcel has not arrived 21 business days after dispatch, the logistics team asks the post
  office to trace it.

## FAQ-RET-01 Returns and exchanges

- Unused items can be returned within 14 days of arrival. The customer pays the postage of a return
  from abroad.
- When an item arrives damaged and the customer sends a photo within 7 days of arrival, we replace
  or refund it at our cost. The person who checks the photo decides which and tells the customer.

## FAQ-PROD-01 Voltage of electrical goods

- Our electrical goods, the KG-12 electric kettle included, are for Japan's 100 V only. They cannot
  be used at 220 V or other voltages abroad, and the warranty does not cover use with a
  transformer.
- We do not currently sell a model for voltages abroad.

## FAQ-BILL-01 Invoices and receipts

- We can issue a PDF receipt for each order, with a company name as the addressee.
- To ask for one, send the order number and the addressee to the support desk. The accounts team
  checks it and sends it by email.
- The support desk does not accept discounts or price changes.
