# DOC-01 Basic purchase agreement (excerpt)

- Parties: Yamagara Shoji Co., Ltd. (buyer) and Shime Seiko Co., Ltd. (seller) (both fictional)
- Signed: 2024-04-01

Article 8 (Delivery date)
The seller delivers the goods by the delivery date of each individual contract. A change of delivery date requires the buyer's and the seller's agreement in writing (email included).

Article 10 (Inspection)
The buyer inspects the goods within 10 days of receiving them, and notifies the seller of any goods that fail.

Article 11 (Failed goods)
On a notice under the previous article, the seller delivers replacements or reduces the price, as the buyer instructs.

Article 12 (Payment)
The buyer pays for goods that passed inspection, closing at the end of each month, by the end of the following month.

Article 15 (Late-payment charges)
A late payment bears charges at 3% a year from the day after the due date until the day of payment.
