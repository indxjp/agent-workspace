# Service Agreement

Misago Systems Co., Ltd. ("Party A") and Yamasemi Data Co., Ltd. ("Party B") enter into this agreement ("this Agreement") on the services Party A entrusts to Party B, as follows.

## Article 1 (Purpose)

Party A entrusts Party B with the maintenance and modification of Party A's core sales system (the "Services"), and Party B accepts them.

## Article 2 (Scope of the Services)

The content, specifications and deadlines of the Services are set in individual contracts agreed between the parties.

## Article 3 (Fees and payment)

1. Party A pays Party B the fees set in the individual contracts as consideration for the Services.
2. Party A pays the fees for deliverables accepted by the end of each month by bank transfer to the account Party B designates, by the end of the month after next.

## Article 4 (Confidentiality)

1. Neither party may disclose or leak to a third party any technical or business information of the other party learned in connection with this Agreement without the other party's written consent.
2. The obligations in this Article survive for three years after this Agreement ends.

## Article 5 (Intellectual property)

1. Copyright (including the rights under Articles 27 and 28 of the Copyright Act) and all other intellectual property rights in the deliverables produced in performing the Services, together with any intellectual property Party B already held that is used in the deliverables, are assigned from Party B to Party A on delivery and belong to Party A.
2. Party B does not exercise its moral rights against Party A.

## Article 6 (Damages)

If Party B breaches this Agreement and causes damage to Party A, Party B compensates Party A for all damages incurred (including lost profits and attorneys' fees).

## Article 7 (Termination)

1. Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party breaches it and fails to cure the breach within a reasonable period after notice.
2. Party A may terminate all or part of this Agreement at any time, for any reason, by notifying Party B.

## Article 8 (Term)

This Agreement is valid for one year from 1 November 2026. After expiry, it is renewed automatically for one further year on the same terms, and likewise thereafter.

## Article 9 (Governing law and jurisdiction)

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Japan. The Tokyo District Court has exclusive jurisdiction in the first instance over any dispute relating to this Agreement.

## Article 10 (Subcontracting)

Party B may subcontract all or part of the Services to a third party only with Party A's prior written consent. Party B then imposes on the subcontractor obligations equal to its own under this Agreement and is responsible to Party A for the subcontractor's acts.

## Article 11 (Consultation)

Matters not set out in this Agreement are resolved by the parties through consultation in good faith.

In witness whereof, two originals of this Agreement are made, signed and sealed by both parties, and each party keeps one.
