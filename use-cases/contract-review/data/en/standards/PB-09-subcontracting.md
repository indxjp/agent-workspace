title: Subcontracting
---

## Standard

- caution: a clause that lets the counterparty subcontract to a third party without the company's consent. An agreement with no subcontracting clause is also caution, and the article in the verdict is "missing".
- OK: a clause that requires the company's prior written consent to subcontract, and makes the counterparty responsible for its subcontractors.

## Standard clause

Party B may subcontract all or part of the Services to a third party only with Party A's prior written consent. Party B then imposes on the subcontractor obligations equal to its own under this Agreement and is responsible to Party A for the subcontractor's acts.
