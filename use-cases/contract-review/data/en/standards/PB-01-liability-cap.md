id: PB-01
title: Liability cap
---

## Standard

- NG: a clause that puts no cap on the damages the company owes, or that widens them to "all damages", "lost profits" or "attorneys' fees".
- OK: a clause that limits damages to ordinary and direct damages and sets a cap. The cap is, as a rule, "the total fees received in the last 12 months". Damages from wilful misconduct or gross negligence may be left outside the cap.

## Standard clause

If either party causes damage to the other in connection with this Agreement, it is liable only for the ordinary and direct damage actually incurred by the other party. The amount of such damages is capped at the total fees paid under this Agreement during the 12 months before the event that caused the damage. This cap does not apply to damage caused by wilful misconduct or gross negligence.
