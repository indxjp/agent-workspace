id: PB-04
title: Intellectual property (background IP)
---

## Standard

- NG: a clause that assigns to the counterparty intellectual property the company held before the agreement or developed independently of it (background IP).
- OK: a clause that transfers only the IP in the deliverables and excludes background IP. An agreement that transfers none of the company's IP is also OK. Background IP built into a deliverable is licensed non-exclusively, as far as using the deliverable requires.

## Standard clause

Copyright (including the rights under Articles 27 and 28 of the Copyright Act) and other intellectual property rights in the deliverables produced in performing the Services (excluding those Party B held before this Agreement and those developed independently of the Services) pass from Party B to Party A when Party A has paid the fees in full. Party B grants Party A, free of charge, a non-exclusive licence to Party B's existing intellectual property contained in the deliverables, as far as Party A needs it to use the deliverables.
