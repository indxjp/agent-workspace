---
id: PB-08
title: Payment due date
---

## Standard

- caution: a due date that can fall more than 60 days after acceptance or delivery ("end of the month after next", "within 90 days of acceptance"). When the company is the buyer, the fix brings it within 60 days.
- OK: a due date within 60 days of acceptance or delivery.

## Standard clause

Party A pays the fees for deliverables accepted by the end of each month by bank transfer to the account Party B designates, by the end of the following month. Party A bears the transfer fees.
