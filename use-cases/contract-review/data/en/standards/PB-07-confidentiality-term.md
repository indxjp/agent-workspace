id: PB-07
title: Survival of confidentiality
---

## Standard

- caution: a clause under which the duty of confidentiality survives "forever" or "without time limit". It makes managing the information too heavy a burden.
- OK: a clause under which the duty survives for a set period after the agreement ends (three years as a rule, five years for technical information).

## Standard clause

The obligations in this Article survive for three years after this Agreement ends. Confidential information that is a trade secret (as defined in Article 2(6) of the Unfair Competition Prevention Act) remains protected for as long as it is a trade secret.
