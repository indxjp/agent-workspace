id: PB-05
title: Automatic renewal and notice of non-renewal
---

## Standard

- caution: a clause that renews automatically with no way to refuse renewal by written notice. The company loses its chance to end the agreement.
- OK: automatic renewal that either party can refuse by written notice before a set period before expiry (three months as a rule, one month at the least). A term with no automatic renewal is also OK.

## Standard clause

This Agreement is valid for one year from the date it is signed. Unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least three months before expiry, it is renewed for one further year on the same terms, and likewise thereafter.
