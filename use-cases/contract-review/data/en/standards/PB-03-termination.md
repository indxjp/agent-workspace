title: Termination
---

## Standard

- NG: a clause that lets only one party terminate "at any time" or "for any reason" (a one-sided right to terminate for convenience).
- OK: grounds for termination that apply equally to both parties. Termination for convenience, if allowed, is allowed to both, on at least three months' written notice, with payment for the work done until termination.

## Standard clause

Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party breaches it and fails to cure the breach within a reasonable period after notice. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other party written notice at least three months in advance; in that case, Party A pays for the part of the work Party B performed by the termination date.
