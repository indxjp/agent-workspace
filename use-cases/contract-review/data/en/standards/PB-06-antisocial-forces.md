---
id: PB-06
title: Exclusion of antisocial forces
---

## Standard

- NG: an agreement with no antisocial-forces clause (a representation and termination without notice). The article in the verdict is "missing".
- OK: a clause in which both parties represent that they are not antisocial forces, with termination without notice if that is breached.

## Standard clause

Each party represents and warrants that neither it nor its officers is, or will in future be, an organised crime group, a member or associate of one, a corporate racketeer or any other antisocial force. If the other party breaches this representation, either party may terminate this Agreement immediately without notice, and the terminating party is not liable for any damage the other party suffers as a result.
