title: Governing law and jurisdiction
---

## Standard

- NG: a clause that names a governing law other than Japanese law (a foreign state or national law), or a foreign court or arbitral body.
- caution: Japanese law governs, but a court other than the Tokyo District Court has exclusive jurisdiction.
- OK: Japanese law governs, and the Tokyo District Court has exclusive jurisdiction in the first instance.

## Standard clause

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Japan. The Tokyo District Court has exclusive jurisdiction in the first instance over any dispute relating to this Agreement.
