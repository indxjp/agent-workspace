# Checking a contract against the review standards

The standards are nine items, one file each in `contract-review/standards/`: `PB-01` liability cap,
`PB-02` governing law and jurisdiction, `PB-03` termination, `PB-04` background IP, `PB-05`
automatic renewal, `PB-06` exclusion of antisocial forces, `PB-07` survival of confidentiality,
`PB-08` payment due date, `PB-09` subcontracting. Each file's rule says which wording is `OK`, which
is caution (`要注意`) and which is `NG`. Apply the rule as written; it is the company's decision, not
yours.

For each item:

1. Find the contract's clause on the same subject, by its heading or its content. A subject the
   contract has nowhere is `missing` (`欠落`); its verdict is what the rule says for an absent
   clause, and `OK` when the rule names none.
2. Compare what the clause says with the rule, point by point. Quote the clause's own words.
3. Take the verdict the rule names for that wording. When the rule names none for it and the clause
   says what the standard clause says, it is `OK`.
4. For caution and `NG`, the suggested fix is the item's standard clause with the contract's own
   party names and defined terms. For `OK`, leave it empty.

Read the whole sentence of a clause: a proviso ("ただし" / "provided that") can change the verdict,
and a clause with several paragraphs is judged on every paragraph (one paragraph granting a
one-sided right is enough for `NG` under `PB-03`). Two subjects in one clause are each judged under
their own item.

When asked to explain one standard, give its rule and its standard clause from its file, in the
language of the request, with the item's id such as `〔PB-04〕`.
