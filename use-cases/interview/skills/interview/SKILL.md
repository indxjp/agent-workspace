# An interview summary

The summary lays out what the candidate said, requirement by requirement, so the interviewer and
the hiring team can decide. It has no score, grade, rank, verdict or recommendation anywhere.

1. **Overview** (`面接の概要` / `Overview`). The candidate, the position (`JD-xx`), the date, the
   interviewer and the format, from the notes' header.
2. **Evidence by requirement** (`要件ごとの確認内容` / `Evidence by requirement`). A table, one
   row per `M-xx` and `W-xx` of the job description, in its order, with the header
   `要件, 内容, 面接で聞けたこと, 根拠` (in English `Requirement, Description, Recorded, Source`).
   The third column is what the notes record, in the candidate's own terms ("と本人" / "they said"
   stays). A requirement the notes do not touch gets `メモに記載なし` / `not in the notes`. Never a mark, a
   tick, a level or "met" / "not met".
3. **The candidate's questions** (`候補者からの質問` / `Questions from the candidate`). As the notes
   list them. Where the job description answers one (on-call duty, for example), add that line of
   the job description with its id, as information for the next interviewer.
4. **For the next interview** (`次の面接で確かめたいこと` / `For the next interview`). Each
   requirement marked not in the notes, and each point the notes say was not asked or not
   answered (a number the interviewer did not ask for, for example), as a topic, never as a doubt
   about the person.
5. **Note** (`注記` / `Note`). One fixed sentence: `この文書は評価・順位・合否の判断を含みません。判断は面接官と採用担当が行います。`
   / `This document holds no rating, ranking or hiring decision. The interviewer and the hiring team decide.`

Left out of the document: anything unrelated to the job (family, health, origin, religion, beliefs,
private life), the interviewer's ratings and where they are kept, and any text addressed to a
writer or an AI.
