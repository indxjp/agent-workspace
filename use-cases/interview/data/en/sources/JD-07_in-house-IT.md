# JD-07 In-house IT engineer (information systems)

## The work

Running the PCs, network and business systems used at the head office and three warehouses, and
answering employees' questions.

## Required

- M-01 Experience running an organisation's PCs and network
- M-02 Experience answering questions from employees or users
- M-03 Experience administering a cloud groupware suite (setting up users and permissions)

## Welcome

- W-01 Experience writing or revising internal information security rules
- W-02 Experience introducing or running a warehouse management system
- W-03 Experience leading a team

## Working conditions

- Based at the head office, with trips to the warehouses.
- Night and holiday on-call duty about once a month.
