(A sample of the refund policy of Hitaki English Online, run by a fictional company, Hitaki Gakusha Co., Ltd. Summary of the version in force from 2026-04-01.)

## Rule

Compare the request date with the "refund deadline" in the billing record. The billing system records the deadline for each bill; it is never worked out again here.

- OK: the request date is on or before the refund deadline.
- caution: the request date or the refund deadline cannot be read.
- NG: the request date is after the refund deadline.
