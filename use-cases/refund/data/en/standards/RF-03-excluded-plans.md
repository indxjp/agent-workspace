id: RF-03
title: Plans that are not refundable
---

(A sample of the refund policy of a fictional company, Hitaki Gakusha Co., Ltd.)

## Rule

Judge by the "refund class" column of the billing record.

- OK: "refundable".
- NG: anything starting with "not refundable" (such as a discounted sign-up campaign plan).
