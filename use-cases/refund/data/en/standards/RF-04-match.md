---
id: RF-04
title: Request and billing record match
---

(A sample of the refund policy of a fictional company, Hitaki Gakusha Co., Ltd.)

## Rule

Compare the request's contract number, billing number and amount with the billing record's row of the same billing number.

- OK: the contract number and the amount match the billing record.
- caution: the contract number or the amount differs from the billing record.
- NG: the billing number is not in the billing record.
