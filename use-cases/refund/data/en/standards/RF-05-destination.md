title: Refund destination
---

(A sample of the refund policy of a fictional company, Hitaki Gakusha Co., Ltd.)

## Rule

A refund goes back to the payment method used.

- OK: the destination is "the payment method used" (a credit card, for example).
- caution: the customer asks for a transfer to another account, or to an account in a name other than the contract holder's. The approver follows the identity check procedure.
