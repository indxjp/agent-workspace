# RET-03 Items that cannot be returned

- Source: returns and exchanges policy, section 4
- Standard: personalised items, underwear and towels once opened, and final clearance items cannot be returned for the customer's own reasons. The "Returnable" column of the order record shows how each item is treated.

## Verdict

- OK: Returnable is "yes".
- Caution: Returnable is "no", but the reason given is a defect, damage or a wrong item (which section 6 may accept).
- NG: Returnable is "no" and the reason is the customer's own.
