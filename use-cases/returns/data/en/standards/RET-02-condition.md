# RET-02 Condition of the item

- Source: returns and exchanges policy, section 3
- Standard: an item can be returned unused, with its tags, box and other accessories.

## Verdict

- OK: the request form's "Condition" is unused (unopened, or opened only) and the accessories are complete.
- Caution: used, but the reason given is a defect or damage (the returned item is inspected to confirm), or no condition is given.
- NG: used, and the reason is the customer's own (not as pictured, no longer needed).
