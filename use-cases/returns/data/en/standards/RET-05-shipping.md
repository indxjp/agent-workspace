# RET-05 Who pays the return shipping

- Source: returns and exchanges policy, section 6
- Standard: the shop pays the return shipping for a defect, damage or a wrong item; the customer pays it for their own reasons. A defect or damage claim needs a photo of the item.

## Verdict

- OK: the reason is the customer's own (the customer pays), or a defect, damage or wrong item with a photo attached (the shop pays).
- Caution: a defect or damage claim with no photo attached, or no reason given.
- NG: this item has no NG.
