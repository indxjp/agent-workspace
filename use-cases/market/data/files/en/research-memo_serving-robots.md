# Research memo: the serving robot market

(A fictional sample. Not related to any real company or organisation.)

- Prepared by: Corporate Planning, market research team (Haruto Hibari)
- Updated: 2026-11-10

## The market covered

Autonomous commercial serving robots that carry food or goods in restaurants, hospitals, care homes and similar places. Household cleaning robots and factory carrying robots (AGVs) are not included.

## Fiscal year and units

- The fiscal year runs from April to March. "FY2025" means April 2025 to March 2026.
- Shipments are given in units, values in JPY billion.

## Terms

- Shipments: domestic shipments that the industry association (Fictional Service Robot Association) totals from its members.
- Production: domestic production that the Fictional Statistics Bureau publishes each quarter. It does not match the shipment figures.

## Note

This team does not make market forecasts from the snapshot of public sources. When a forecast is needed, consider commissioning a research firm.
