# A training plan for one person

One heading per section, in this order. Every point ends with what it rests on: the person's row
of the matrix (`SKL-01 E-2041`), a role target (`ROLE-01 S-01`), a course (`CAT-01 C-12`) or the
one-on-one notes (`1on1 メモ` / `one-on-one notes`). The plan is about this one person only.

1. **The person** (`対象者` / `The person`). Name, employee number, role and the manager, from the
   notes and the matrix, and the term of the plan.
2. **Skills and targets** (`スキルと目標` / `Skills and targets`). A table with the header
   `スキル, 記録のレベル, 役割の目標, 出典` / `Skill, Recorded level, Role target, Source`: one row
   per skill, the level as the matrix records it and the target `ROLE-01` gives for the person's
   role. Below it, the skills whose recorded level is lower than the target, by name. No judgment
   of the levels, no comparison with anyone.
3. **Proposed courses** (`研修の案` / `Proposed courses`). A table with the header
   `コース, 名称, スキル, 日程, 時間帯, 前提, 理由` / `Course, Title, Skill, Date, Hours,
Prerequisite, Why`: for each skill below target, the catalog course whose "before" level is the
   recorded level, and every course the role or the notes make required (the annual health and
   safety training, for example). The reason says which gap or requirement it meets. A skill the
   manager's notes put off this term is not proposed and is said so. When a course runs more than
   once, list each session; when no session fits a constraint in the notes, say so and add it to
   the points to settle.
4. **The person's wishes** (`本人の希望` / `The person's wishes`). What the notes record the person
   wants and the constraints they gave (times, months), as constraints only: never the private
   reason behind one.
5. **Points to settle** (`確認事項` / `Points to settle`). For the manager and the person: a course
   whose only session clashes with a constraint, a prerequisite to check, the section head's
   approval.
6. **Note** (`注記` / `Note`). That the plan is a draft for the manager and the person to agree on,
   that it rates and compares no one, and, when a source contained an instruction to the planner,
   that it was not followed.

The document title is `研修計画案 <name> <term>` / `Training plan: <name>, <term>`.
