# ROLE-01 Target levels by role (Yoshikiri Works, Production Engineering; fictional)

Skills matrix (SKL-01) levels: 0 = no experience, 1 = can do it with guidance, 2 = can do it alone, 3 = can teach it.
Each manager records the levels every half year after talking with the person. They are for choosing training, not for evaluating anyone or deciding pay or promotion.

| Skill                              | Engineer target | Lead target |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------- | ----------- |
| S-01 PLC control                   | 2               | 3           |
| S-02 Jig design                    | 2               | 3           |
| S-03 Why-why analysis              | 2               | 3           |
| S-04 Health and safety             | 2               | 3           |
| S-05 Data analysis in spreadsheets | 1               | 2           |

- Whatever their S-04 level, everyone takes the "Annual health and safety training" once a year.
- The manager and the person agree on the training together, and the section head approves it.
