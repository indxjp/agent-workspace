(A fictional sample counterparty screening procedure.)

## Covers

Every name in the registration request's "Company name" and "Former or trade names".

## How to judge

Check each name against the snapshot of the public lists. The same name as a listed name or alias is a "match"; a shared distinctive word, a variant spelling or a different legal suffix is "review"; nothing found by any search is "no match". State the date of the lists checked.
