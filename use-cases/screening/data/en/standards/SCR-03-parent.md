title: Parent company and major shareholders

(A fictional sample counterparty screening procedure.)

## Covers

Every name under "Parent company" and "Major shareholders (10% or more)" in the registration request.

## How to judge

Also check each company name without its legal suffix (KK, Ltd., Holdings and the like) and by its distinctive word alone. When part of the name is the same but it looks like a different entity, do not conclude it is unrelated: mark it "review".
