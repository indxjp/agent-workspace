(A fictional sample counterparty screening procedure.)

## Covers

Every person named under "Officers" and "Beneficial owners" in the registration request.

## How to judge

Check each name in romaji and in katakana. Katakana spellings vary, so also search the family name in romaji on its own. When the list gives a date of birth and the request does not, do not decide whether it is the same person: mark it "review" and name the date of birth as the point to confirm.
