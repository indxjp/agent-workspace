title: End user and ship-to address
---

(A fictional sample counterparty screening procedure.)

## Covers

The name, country and city under "End user" and "Ship-to address" in the registration request.

## How to judge

Check the end user's name, and search the ship-to country or city to see every listed record there. The same country or city alone is never a "match": judge by the result for the name.
