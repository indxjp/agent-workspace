# Shipping and delivery times

## Shipping fee

- Shipping is free when one order totals JPY 4,000 or more, tax included.
- Below JPY 4,000, shipping is a flat JPY 550 (tax included) anywhere in Japan.
- Subscription deliveries ship free, whatever the amount.

## Delivery times

- We ship within 3 business days of the order being confirmed, and email you the day it ships.
- Delivery takes about 1 to 2 days to Honshu, Shikoku and Kyushu, and 2 to 4 days to Hokkaido, Okinawa and the outlying islands.
- You can choose a delivery date from 5 to 14 days after the order is confirmed.

## Tracking a parcel

Use the tracking number in the shipping email on the carrier's website. The help center and the chat cannot look up the status of an order.
