# Returns and exchanges

## Returns for your own reasons

- Only unopened items can be returned, if you tell us within 8 days of delivery.
- You pay the return shipping.
- Opened beans and ground coffee cannot be returned, because their quality can no longer be kept.

## Damaged or wrong items

- If an item arrives damaged, contains foreign matter, or is not what you ordered, contact us through the contact form within 8 days of delivery. We exchange or refund it, even when opened.
- We pay the shipping in this case.
- Include the order number and a photo showing the item's condition.

## When refunds arrive

We refund to the payment method of the order within 5 business days of the returned item reaching us.
