# Payment methods

## Accepted payment methods

- Credit card (VISA, Mastercard, JCB, American Express)
- Pay later at a convenience store (JPY 250 fee, tax included; pay within 14 days of the invoice)
- Cash on delivery is not available.

## Paying for a subscription

Subscriptions are paid by credit card only. Change the card on My Page under "Payment method".

## Please note

Never write your card number in an email, the chat or the contact form. We never ask for your card number.
