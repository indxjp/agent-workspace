# Contact us

## Contact form

For anything the help-center articles do not solve, and for the status of a specific order (shipping, refunds, billing), send us the contact form.

- Form: https://help.yamagara-coffee.example/en/contact
- Hours: weekdays 10:00 to 17:00 (closed weekends, public holidays and the New Year break). Messages sent outside these hours are answered in turn from the next business day.
- Reply: by email within 2 business days.

## What to include

Your order number (it starts with "YC-") and the email address you registered with.
