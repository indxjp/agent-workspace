# Changing, skipping or cancelling a subscription

## Deadline

Change a delivery, skip the next one or cancel on My Page by 23:59, 5 days before the next shipping date. After the deadline the next delivery is being prepared and cannot be changed.

## Skipping just the next delivery

On My Page, open "Subscription" and choose "Skip next delivery". You can skip up to 2 deliveries in a row. Skipping is free.

## Cancelling

- On My Page, open "Subscription" and choose "Cancel".
- A subscription can be cancelled from the second delivery on. It cannot be cancelled after the first delivery only.
- Cancelling is free.
