# GUIDE-01 How we write internal slides (Aoji Denso, General Affairs)

1. Slide 1 is the cover: the deck's name, the department and the date of the talk.
2. Slide 2 is the "Conclusion": at most 3 things this report wants to say.
3. One message per slide. A slide's title states that message as a sentence (for example "Every request now comes through the form").
4. At most 5 bullets on a slide. When they do not fit, split the slide.
5. Every figure names its source (which document) and its date. Never show a figure the source does not have (a total, a growth rate).
6. Never hide a delay or a problem: give the reason and the outlook.
7. The last slide is "Asks": what the audience should do.
8. No photos or illustrations (so that no confidential screen ends up in a deck).
