---
id: PG-04
title: Quote validity
---

(A sample of the pricing and terms rules of a fictional company, Mimizuku Instruments Co., Ltd.)

## Rule

Judge the validity printed on the quote.

- OK: "30 days from the issue date".
- caution: no validity stated.
- NG: a validity with no end, such as "no expiry" or "valid until ordered".

## Action

For caution and NG, state the validity as "30 days from the issue date".
