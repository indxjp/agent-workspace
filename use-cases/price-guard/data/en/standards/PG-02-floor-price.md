id: PG-02
title: Floor price
---

(A sample of the pricing and terms rules of a fictional company, Mimizuku Instruments Co., Ltd.)

## Rule

For each quote line, compare the offered unit price with the floor price of the same part number in the price list.

- OK: the offered unit price is at or above the floor price.
- caution: the part number is not in the price list.
- NG: the offered unit price is below the floor price, with or without an approval number.

## Action

For NG, raise the offered unit price to at least the floor price in the price list. For caution, confirm the part number and its floor price with product planning.
