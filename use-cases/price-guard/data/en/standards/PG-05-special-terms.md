---
id: PG-05
title: Free items and special conditions
---

(A sample of the pricing and terms rules of a fictional company, Mimizuku Instruments Co., Ltd.)

## Rule

Judge whether the quote's lines or remarks offer anything free of charge or a special condition that the price list does not have (a free extension of support, extra free work, a special return clause).

- OK: nothing free and no special condition; or the quote carries the sales manager's approval number covering it.
- caution: something free or a special condition, with no approval number covering it.

## Action

For caution, get the sales manager's approval and print its number on the quote.
