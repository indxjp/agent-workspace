title: Payment terms
---

(A sample of the pricing and terms rules of a fictional company, Mimizuku Instruments Co., Ltd.)

## Rule

Judge the payment terms printed on the quote.

- OK: "end of month, paid end of next month" (bank transfer).
- caution: "end of month, paid end of the month after next" (bank transfer).
- NG: anything else (promissory notes, longer than the end of the month after next, or none stated).

## Action

For caution, get the sales manager's approval. For NG, change the terms to "end of month, paid end of next month", or consult accounting and the sales manager.
