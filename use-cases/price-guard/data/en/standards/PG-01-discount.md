title: Discount rate
---

(A sample of the pricing and terms rules of a fictional company, Mimizuku Instruments Co., Ltd. Summary of the version revised on 2026-10-01.)

## Rule

Judge each quote line by the discount rate printed on the quote.

- OK: the discount rate is 15% or less; or it is over 15% and up to 25%, and the quote carries the sales manager's approval number covering that line.
- caution: the discount rate is over 15% and up to 25%, with no approval number covering that line.
- NG: the discount rate is over 25%, with or without an approval number.

## Action

For caution, get the sales manager's approval and print its number on the quote. For NG, bring the rate to 25% or less and consult the sales manager.
