# Cannot connect to the VPN

To use internal systems from outside the office, connect with the VPN app "Soramichi Connect". When an error appears, follow the steps for its error code.

## Error E-201 "Authentication failed"

1. Right after changing your password, also change the password saved in Soramichi Connect's settings to the new one.
2. Approve the sign-in notification sent to the authenticator app on your smartphone.

## Error E-305 "Cannot reach the server"

1. Check that other websites open, to confirm you are connected to the internet.
2. On hotel or café Wi-Fi, finish the Wi-Fi login page in the browser first, then connect.
3. Check that the destination is vpn-tokyo.mozuya.example.

## Error E-412 "The certificate has expired"

1. The device certificate must be renewed. Come to the office, connect to the office network, and press "Renew certificate" in the "Device Manager" app.
2. When you cannot come to the office, contact the helpdesk (KB-0105).
