# Lost PC or smartphone

## What to do at once

1. As soon as you notice the loss, call the loss line 03-0000-7712, at any hour. IT locks the device and, when needed, erases its data.
2. Report the loss to your manager.
3. When you lost it outside the office, report it to the nearest police station and give IT the report number.

## When it is found

Contact the helpdesk (KB-0105). IT checks the device before it is put back into use.
