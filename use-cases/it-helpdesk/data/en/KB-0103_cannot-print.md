# Cannot print on the multifunction printer

The office multifunction printers use "Secure Print". A print job sent from your PC waits on the printer and comes out when you hold your staff card to the printer.

## The printer does not appear in the print dialog

1. Open "Software Center" on your PC and install "Multifunction printer driver".
2. Restart the PC and check that "Secure Print" appears as a printer.

## Nothing comes out when you hold your staff card

1. A job you sent is deleted after 12 hours. When 12 hours have passed, print it again from your PC.
2. The first time you use your staff card, register it with "Register card" on the printer's screen by entering your employee number.

## When you still cannot print

Contact the helpdesk (KB-0105) and give the device number on the front of the printer (for example, MFP-3E).
