# Forgotten or locked password

## Reset your password

1. Open the password reset portal at https://password.mozuya.example.
2. Enter your employee number.
3. Enter the 6-digit confirmation code sent by SMS to your registered mobile phone.
4. Set a new password. It must be at least 12 characters and contain at least one upper-case letter, one lower-case letter and one digit. None of your last 5 passwords can be used.

## When your account is locked

Five wrong passwords in a row lock the account for 30 minutes. Wait 30 minutes and sign in again, or reset your password with the steps above to unlock it.

## When no mobile phone is registered

Contact the helpdesk (KB-0105). After checking who you are, they issue a temporary password.

## Note

IT and the helpdesk never ask for your password by phone, email or chat. Never tell anyone your password.
