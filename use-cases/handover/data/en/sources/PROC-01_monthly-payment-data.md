# PROC-01 Preparing the monthly payment data

By the 5th business day of each month, prepare the previous month's payment data and pass it to
Accounts.

1. Export the list of orders accepted last month from the purchasing system as CSV.
2. Match each order's amount and quantity against the supplier's invoice.
3. Leave out any order with a gap and ask the supplier about it (add it to the task list).
4. Save the matched orders in the shared folder "Purchasing/Payment data/<year-month>".
5. Tell the Accounts contact (Saya Migiwa) in chat that the data is saved.
