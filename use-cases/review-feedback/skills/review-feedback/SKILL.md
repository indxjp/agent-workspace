# Review comments for one person

The company's guide (`GL-01`) decides how comments are written; where it and this skill differ,
the guide wins. Every point ends with what it rests on: the manager's notes or the self-assessment
with the goal (`上長メモ G-01` / `manager's notes G-01`, `自己申告 G-03` / `self-assessment G-03`),
or a guide item (`GL-01 8`). Write to the person, plainly and specifically.

1. **The person and the term** (`対象者と期間` / `Person and term`). Name, section, reviewer and the
   term, from the goal sheet.
2. **Comments per goal** (`目標ごとのコメント` / `Comments per goal`). A subheading per goal
   (`G-01 <goal>`), and under it, in the guide's order: what the person did and what came of it,
   with the figures as recorded (both the result and the goal's measure, side by side, without a
   verdict or a rate); the behaviour that went well, as an action the notes record; what to aim for
   next term, drawn from the notes. A goal that missed its deadline says so, with its reason and
   what the person did about it.
3. **Next term** (`次の期に向けて` / `Next term`). At most three points, each tied to a goal above.
4. **Points to check** (`確認事項` / `Points to check`). For the reviewer to check with the person:
   each place the manager's notes and the self-assessment differ (both versions, side by side),
   and anything the notes leave unknown.
5. **Note** (`注記` / `Note`). That the comments are a draft for the reviewer, that the reviewer
   decides the rating at the review meeting and the comments give none, and, when the notes
   contained an instruction to the writer, that an instruction in the notes was not followed,
   without repeating what it asked for (never the rating it named).

The document title is `評価コメント案 <name> <term>` / `Review comments: <name>, <term>`.
