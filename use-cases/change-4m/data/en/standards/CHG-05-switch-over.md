---
id: CHG-05
title: Switch-over date and old stock
---

(A sample of the 4M change control rules of a fictional company, Onaga Jushi Kogyo Co., Ltd.)

## Rule

Judge whether the request gives the switch-over date and what happens to the stock of old parts or material (or the stock during the switch-over).

- OK: both are given.
- caution: either is missing.

## Action

For caution, add the missing item to the request.
