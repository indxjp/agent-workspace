---
id: CHG-03
title: First-lot inspection
---

(A sample of the 4M change control rules of a fictional company, Onaga Jushi Kogyo Co., Ltd.)

## Rule

Judge whether the request gives the date and the quantity of a first-lot inspection of the first lot after the change.

- OK: the inspection date and quantity are given.
- NG: no first-lot inspection is given, or its date or quantity is missing.

## Action

For NG, agree the inspection date and quantity with quality assurance and add them to the request.
