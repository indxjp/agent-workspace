title: Kind of change
---

(A sample of the 4M change control rules of a fictional company, Onaga Jushi Kogyo Co., Ltd. Summary of the version revised on 2026-07-01.)

## Rule

Judge the "kind of change" on the request.

- OK: one of man, machine, material or method is stated.
- caution: no kind is stated.

## Action

For caution, the requester states the kind and submits the request again.
