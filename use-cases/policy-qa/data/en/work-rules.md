# Work Rules

## Chapter 3 Working hours

### Article 15 (Start, end and breaks)

1. The working day starts at 9:00 a.m. and ends at 6:00 p.m.
2. The break is 60 minutes, from noon to 1:00 p.m.
3. A manager may move the start and end times earlier or later for business reasons.

### Article 16 (Overtime)

1. Overtime is worked only on the manager's prior instruction or approval.
2. Overtime is requested in the attendance system by 5:00 p.m. on the day.

## Chapter 4 Leave

### Article 18 (Annual paid leave)

1. An employee who has worked continuously for six months from the joining date is granted 10 days of annual paid leave.
2. To take annual paid leave, request it in the attendance system as a rule at least 3 business days before the day, and get the manager's approval.
3. When a sudden illness or another unavoidable reason prevents a request in advance, request it within 2 business days of returning to work.

### Article 20 (Leave for family events)

1. The following special leave (paid) is granted on request.

| Event                                                      | Days   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| The employee's own wedding                                 | 5 days |
| The birth of a child to the employee's spouse              | 2 days |
| The death of a spouse, child or parent                     | 5 days |
| The death of a grandparent, a sibling or a spouse's parent | 3 days |

2. Leave for a family event is taken within 30 days of the event.

## Chapter 5 Working from home

### Article 22 (Working from home)

1. An employee who joined at least 3 months ago may work from home up to 3 days a week, with the manager's approval.
2. On a day at home, clock in and out in the attendance system at the start and end of the day.
3. Work from home only on a device the company lends. Never use a personal device (IT Use Rules, Article 6).
