# IT Use Rules

### Article 6 (Devices)

1. Use only a device the company lends for work.
2. Never store work data on a personal device.

### Article 8 (Passwords)

1. A password has at least 12 characters and is not used for any other service.
2. Set up multi-factor authentication on the company's systems.

### Article 11 (Outside services)

1. Never enter information classified confidential or above into an outside service, a generative AI service included.
2. The outside services IT allows are listed on the company portal under "Approved services".

### Article 14 (Loss and theft)

1. When a company device is lost or stolen, tell IT (extension 2300) within one hour of noticing.
2. IT then locks the device remotely and erases its data.
