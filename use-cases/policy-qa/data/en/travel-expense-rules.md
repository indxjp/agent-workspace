# Travel and Expense Rules

## Chapter 2 Business trips

### Article 6 (Trip requests)

1. Before a business trip, file a trip request in the expense system at least 5 business days before departure, and get the manager's approval.
2. A trip abroad is requested at least 20 business days before departure, with the department head's approval.

### Article 8 (Hotels)

1. For a domestic trip, the hotel is paid at cost up to the following amount per night.

| Area                         | Limit (per night, tax included) |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Tokyo's 23 wards, Osaka city | JPY 12,000                      |
| Everywhere else              | JPY 10,000                      |

2. A hotel above the limit is allowed only when the reason is written in the trip request and the department head approves it.

### Article 9 (Daily allowance)

1. The daily allowance for a domestic trip is JPY 2,000 per day, for a trip of 100 km or more one way.
2. A day trip of less than 100 km one way gets no daily allowance.

## Chapter 3 Expense claims

### Article 12 (Deadline)

1. Claim an expense in the expense system by the 10th of the month after the month it was paid.
2. A late claim is accepted only with a written reason from the manager.

### Article 13 (Receipts)

1. Attach an image of the receipt to the claim. For transport with no receipt, write the route and the amount.

### Article 15 (Entertainment)

1. A meal with people from outside the company should stay within JPY 5,000 per person, tax included.
2. When it is expected to exceed JPY 5,000 per person, get the department head's approval in advance.
