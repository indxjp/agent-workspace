# Summarising an application against the requirements

The requirements are five files in `screening-hr/standards/`: `REQ-01` hands-on production
engineering or maintenance experience, `REQ-02` reading and revising mechanical drawings, `REQ-03`
improvement work with quality methods, `REQ-04` revising PLC programs, `REQ-05` reading English
technical documents. Each file says whether the requirement is required or preferred, and how to
judge it. Apply that as written; it is the hiring team's rule, not yours.

For each requirement:

1. Find the parts of the application that speak to it: the work history, the duties, the training
   and certificates. Quote the applicant's own words.
2. Take the verdict the file's "how to judge" names for what the application says:
   - `met` (`満たす`): the application states what the requirement asks for;
   - `unclear` (`不明確`): the application mentions the subject, but not enough to tell;
   - `not shown` (`記載なし`): the application says nothing about it, or states less than it asks
     for (fewer years, for example). This is a fact about the document, not about the person.
3. For `unclear` and `not shown`, write what a person could ask the applicant to confirm.

Copy the years of experience exactly as the application states them; do not add up periods or
compute an age. A summary has no total, no count of requirements met, no score and no order of
applicants: the person reading it weighs the requirements.
