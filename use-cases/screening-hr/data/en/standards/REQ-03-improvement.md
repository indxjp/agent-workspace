# REQ-03 Improvement work with quality methods

- Type: required
- Requirement: experience leading improvements that reduced defects or equipment stops, using methods such as 5 Whys, the seven QC tools or FMEA.

## How to judge

- Met: both the method used and what was improved (defects, stops, a process) are described.
- Unclear: "took part in improvement activities", with either the method or the applicant's own role missing.
- Not shown: no improvement work is described.
