# REQ-02 Reading and revising mechanical drawings

- Type: required
- Requirement: experience reading mechanical drawings (part and assembly drawings) in 2D CAD and revising dimensions or tolerances.

## How to judge

- Met: a concrete task of revising or creating drawings in CAD is described.
- Unclear: "handled drawings" or "used CAD", with no way to tell whether the applicant only read them or also revised them.
- Not shown: nothing about drawings or CAD.
