# Offboarding checklist (Hashibiro Kogyo, fictional)

The steps HR and IT follow when an employee leaves. The leavers are in the HR records `leavers.csv`, the items lent to them in `equipment-loans.csv`, and their current access in `current-access.csv`, exported from access management.

## OFF-01 Returns

- List the items in `equipment-loans.csv`, one per asset number.
- They go back to General Affairs by the last working day. General Affairs records each return.

## OFF-02 Handover

- Name the person taking over and the manager from the HR records. The manager and the person taking over decide the task list and what is handed over.

## OFF-03 Removing access

- Record each access in `current-access.csv` as a removal (revoke) in access management, one level on one system at a time.
- Access stays usable until the last working day: write the last working day as `until`.
- Each removal is recorded only after an IT staff member approves it.

## OFF-04 Accounts and data

- Deleting the account itself, and erasing mail or file data, is not part of this procedure.
- IT does that by a person's decision once the data retention period has passed.

## OFF-05 What is never recorded

- The reason for leaving, any assessment of the person and whether they may be rehired are recorded nowhere in this procedure. HR handles them separately.

## OFF-06 One at a time

- One removal is one level on one system. Never record several together.
