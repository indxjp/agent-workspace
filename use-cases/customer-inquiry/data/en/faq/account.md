## FAQ-ACC-01 When do points expire?

Points expire 1 year after your last purchase. Any purchase within that period extends the expiry of all your points.

## FAQ-ACC-02 I want to change my registered address or email address

Changing your registered address, phone number or email address needs an identity check. The person in charge checks your identity by phone and then makes the change.

## FAQ-ACC-03 I want to pause or cancel my subscription

You can skip a delivery or cancel your subscription from "Manage subscription" on My Page up to 10 days before the next delivery date. After that date, that delivery is sent.
