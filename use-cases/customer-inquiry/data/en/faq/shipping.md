## FAQ-SHIP-01 How long does delivery take?

Items in stock are delivered 2 to 4 days after you order (except remote islands and some areas). We email you the tracking number when the item ships.

## FAQ-SHIP-02 Only part of my order arrived

Depending on stock, we sometimes send an order in several parcels. The rest is sent as soon as it is in stock, with no extra shipping charge, and we email you when it ships.

## FAQ-SHIP-03 How much is shipping?

Shipping is free for an order of 5,000 yen (tax included) or more. Below 5,000 yen, it is a flat 550 yen anywhere in Japan. Subscription prices include shipping.
