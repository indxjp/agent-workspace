## FAQ-RET-01 Can I return an item?

We accept returns within 8 days of delivery, for unopened and unused items only. To return an item, apply from the order history on My Page or through the enquiry form. We will send you a return slip. When you return an item for your own reasons, the return shipping is at your expense.

## FAQ-RET-02 The item arrived broken or does not work (initial defect)

An initial defect that appears within 30 days of delivery is exchanged for the same item free of charge. We collect the defective item when the replacement arrives. If the item is out of stock, the person in charge will tell you what happens next.

## FAQ-RET-03 When will I get my refund?

We start the refund 3 to 5 business days after the returned item reaches us and we have checked its condition. For credit card payments, the actual refund date depends on your card company's closing date. Whether a refund is made, and its amount, are checked individually by the person in charge.
