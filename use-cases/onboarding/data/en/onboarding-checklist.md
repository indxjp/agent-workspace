# Onboarding checklist (Mizunara Denki, a fictional company)

HR keeps this list of what to prepare for each person who joins. For each new hire, create one task for each of ONB-01 to ONB-06 below. The due date is the one the "Due" column names, taken from `new-hires.csv` and `training-schedule.csv`.

| Item   | Task                           | Owner                                      | Due                                                   | What                                                                                          |
| ------ | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ONB-01 | Issue the accounts             | IT, Kei Shishido                           | Start date                                            | Issue the accounts that `policies/account-policy.md` lists.                                   |
| ONB-02 | Prepare the PC and equipment   | IT, Kei Shishido                           | Start date                                            | Prepare what `policies/equipment-policy.md` lists and deliver it to the work location.        |
| ONB-03 | Prepare the badge and desk     | General Affairs, Misato Kaji               | Start date                                            | Issue the building badge for the work location and prepare a desk.                            |
| ONB-04 | First-day orientation          | HR, Haruka Usui                            | The orientation date in `training-schedule.csv`       | Work rules, time keeping and expenses.                                                        |
| ONB-05 | Register the required training | HR, Haruka Usui                            | Start date                                            | Register for the security training and the compliance training; put their dates in the notes. |
| ONB-06 | First-week check-in            | The manager (`manager` in `new-hires.csv`) | The first-week review date in `training-schedule.csv` | A meeting of the manager and the new hire about questions and next week's plan.               |

The task title is "[item] task: name (joins start date)". For example: `[ONB-01] Issue the accounts: Akari Shigihara (joins 2026-12-01)`.
