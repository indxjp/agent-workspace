# Account policy (fictional)

## Issued to everyone

- Email
- Team chat
- Time and attendance system

## By employment type

- Full-time: also the expense system.
- Fixed-term contract: no expense system (the manager files their expenses).

## By department

| Department      | Also issued                |
| --------------- | -------------------------- |
| Sales Planning  | Customer management system |
| Engineering     | Source code management     |
| General Affairs | Document management system |

No administrator rights are issued at the start.
