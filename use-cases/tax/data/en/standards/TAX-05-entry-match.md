---
id: TAX-05
title: Match with the entry
---

(A sample of the accounting manual of a fictional company, Komadori Seisakusho Co., Ltd.)

## Rule

Compare the "voucher total (incl. tax)" on the entry rows of the same invoice number with the invoice's "total (incl. tax)".

- OK: there are rows for the invoice number, and the voucher total equals the invoice total.
- NG: there are no rows for the invoice number, or the voucher total differs from the invoice total.

## Action

For NG, the person who typed the entry corrects it from the invoice.
