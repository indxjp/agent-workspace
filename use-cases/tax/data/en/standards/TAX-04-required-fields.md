title: Issuer, date and addressee
---

(A sample of the accounting manual of a fictional company, Komadori Seisakusho Co., Ltd. It does not state how anything is treated for tax.)

## Rule

Judge whether the invoice shows the issuer's name, the transaction date (the invoice date) and the addressee (our company's name).

- OK: all three are shown.
- caution: any one is missing.

## Action

For caution, ask the supplier for an invoice with the missing item filled in.
