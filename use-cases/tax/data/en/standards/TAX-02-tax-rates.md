title: Tax rates
---

(A sample of the accounting manual of a fictional company, Komadori Seisakusho Co., Ltd. It does not state how anything is treated for tax.)

## Rule

Judge the invoice's lines and totals.

- OK: every line shows its tax rate (10% or 8%), and the invoice shows, per rate, the amount it applies to and the tax.
- caution: a line has no tax rate, or the amount or the tax per rate is missing.

## Action

For caution, ask the supplier for an invoice that shows them per rate, and pass it to the accounting team.
