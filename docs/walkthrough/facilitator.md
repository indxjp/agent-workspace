# Facilitator rules

The facilitator answers nothing about how to complete a step. They may repeat the participant text
verbatim, supply only the prepared test values, and intervene only for safety or to stop destructive
external effects.

Every question, hesitation, wrong turn, or request for explanation is recorded in the confusion log
and filed as an issue. The issue title starts with `W8 walkthrough · step N` and links back to the
matching confusion-log id. Do not coach the participant and then report the coached result as an
unassisted completion.
