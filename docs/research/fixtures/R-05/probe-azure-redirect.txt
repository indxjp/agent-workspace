### A. what `init.redirect` is, per client configuration, on chat.completions.create

  AzureOpenAI + apiKey
    {"redirect":"manual","api-key header":true,"authorization header":false}
  AzureOpenAI + azureADTokenProvider
    {"redirect":"(unset)","api-key header":false,"authorization header":true}
  plain OpenAI, baseURL {endpoint}/openai/v1, defaultHeaders {api-key}
    {"redirect":"(unset)","api-key header":true,"authorization header":true}

### B. where the behaviour lives: the only two redirect assignments in openai/azure.js
  azure.js:89: built.req.redirect = 'manual';
  azure.js:95: init.redirect = 'manual';
  enclosing methods: AzureOpenAI.buildRequest() and AzureOpenAI.fetchWithAuth(),
  both guarded by `headers.has("api-key")`. The base OpenAI class overrides neither.
