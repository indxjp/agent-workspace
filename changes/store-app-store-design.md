- The Agent Store (`examples/store`) looks and reads like an app store: an icon per agent on its
  area's colour, tiles with a Try button, a featured card, a four-step "How it works" band (find,
  try, make it yours, install in your cloud), a "What this store shows" band whose handwritten
  line counts come from the apps benchmark, and a detail page with a facts row, safety labels and
  the steps from the page to a company's own cloud. Every count still comes from the catalog.
